# Research Summary

**Title:** Predictors of Language Anxiety in Oral Communication of Grade 12 Students of Mabini Colleges, Inc.
**Authors:** Alma Doloeras, Abegail Gallano, Michelle Gruba, Princess Angelica Padillo
**Institution:** Mabini Colleges, Inc. | Department of Education | RES+ 2025–2026

---

## Objectives

1. Determine the level of English language anxiety (Communication Anxiety, Fear of Negative Evaluation, Test Anxiety, English Class Anxiety)
2. Assess the degree of existence of predictors (Self-confidence, Language Proficiency, Peer Influence, Classroom Environment)
3. Identify the major predictors of language anxiety in oral communication
4. Propose evidence-based interventions

## Methodology

- **Design:** Quantitative, Descriptive-Correlational
- **Respondents:** n = 237 Grade 12 students (population = 617; Cochran's formula)
- **Sampling:** Stratified Random Sampling by strand (STEM, ABM, HUMSS, TVL, GAS)
- **Instrument:** Researcher-made survey questionnaire (expert-validated, Cronbach's Alpha reliability)
- **Statistics:** Weighted Mean, Percentage, Pearson r (SPSS), Ranking

## Major Findings

1. **High** Communication Anxiety (2.92) and Fear of Negative Evaluation (2.79)
2. **Moderate** Test Anxiety (2.25) and English Class Anxiety (2.38)
3. Strongest predictor: **Self-Confidence** (2.52, Agree)
4. Null hypothesis largely accepted — no significant anxiety–predictor correlations
5. Only significant: Self-Confidence × Language Proficiency (r=−.896, p=.039)

## Proposed Interventions

1. **Progressive Oral Communication Activities** — guided pair work, role-playing, low-stakes tasks
2. **Error-tolerant Feedback Strategies** — constructive feedback, peer encouragement, safe classroom norms
3. **Alternative Assessment Methods** — oral portfolios, formative assessments, multiple practice opportunities
4. **Confidence-Enhancement Programs** — speaking practice, goal-setting, cooperative learning strategies
